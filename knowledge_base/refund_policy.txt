Refund Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund if:
- The order was not delivered within 7 business days of the promised delivery date.
- The product received was damaged, defective, or incorrect.
- The customer cancels the order before it is shipped.

Refunds for orders under $50 can be processed automatically by the system.
Refunds for orders $50 or above require human approval before processing.
Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days after approval.
