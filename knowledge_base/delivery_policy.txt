Delivery Policy

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within the country.
Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and incurs an additional fee.
If an order is delayed beyond 7 business days from the order date, the customer is eligible to request a refund or replacement.
Delivery delays caused by courier issues, weather, or customs are covered under this policy.
Customers should contact support if their order status shows "Delayed" for more than 3 days past the expected delivery date.
